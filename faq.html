Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about Read Now

Student Questions

How long does progress take?

Progress will vary depending on the amount of time the student devotes to the effort. Each session lasts from 30-40 minutes. We currently average an increase of one reading grade level every 3 sessions.

How do I get started?

Call our phone number or email us and we will conduct an initial interview to determine if you are a good fit for our services. Students with reading problems will be paired with a tutor to start the training process that will result in the silent reading skill.

Are sessions one-on-one?

Yes! All our Read Now sessions are one on one with a student and a volunteer tutor.

How much does it cost?

All Read Now services are provided at no charge.

Who can participate?

Read Now students are those who wish to remediate a reading problem. Read Now defines a reading problem as a situation where the student can understand a text when it is read aloud to them, but struggles to read that same text silently.

Can I do remote sessions?

Yes! All tutoring sessions are conducted using the Zoom platform. Students must therefore have internet access and download the Zoom app when necessary.

Where do sessions take place?

Sessions can take place in person at various Austin Public Library branches or via video call. Session location is arranged between you and your tutor.

Volunteer Questions

What are the volunteer requirements?

To become a volunteer tutor, you must be 18+ years old, have a desire to make an impact, and consent to a background check.

Do I need teaching experience?

No teaching experience is necessary! We provide free training in our unique Read Now methodology. As long as you have a passion for helping others, we can train you to be an effective tutor.

What kind of training do I receive?

Volunteers receive free training and ongoing supervision from Read Now staff. We'll teach you our specific methodology and provide support throughout your volunteer work.

How flexible are volunteer hours?

Very flexible! You arrange session times directly with your student. Whether you can commit to one session a week or multiple sessions, we work with your schedule.

How long is the volunteer commitment?

The commitment is flexible and based on your personal schedule. Some volunteers work with one student; others work with multiple students.

Do volunteers receive a background check?

Yes, all volunteers consent to a background check as part of the application process to ensure the safety of our students.
