Volunteer Tutor Code of Conduct

Standards for professional and ethical behavior

All Read Now volunteer tutors agree to maintain high standards of professional and ethical conduct. By volunteering with Read Now, you commit to the following principles:

Confidentiality

Volunteer tutors will assiduously keep confidential from those outside Read Now all information concerning Read Now clients, except as required by law.

Professional Boundaries

Volunteer tutors will refrain from all contact with Read Now clients except those contacts directly related to services provided by Read Now.

No Material Compensation

Volunteer tutors will accept no material compensation for their activities performed for Read Now and Read Now clients.

Gift Restrictions

Volunteer tutors will accept no gifts over $25 in value from any Read Now client. Volunteer tutors will accept no gifts of any kind from anyone in any way related to a Read Now client.

Professional Relationships

Social and/or sexual relationships between Read Now volunteer tutors and Read Now clients are strictly prohibited.

Approved Methods

Volunteer tutors will refrain from using training methods, materials and locations other than those approved by Read Now with any Read Now client.

Transportation

Volunteer tutors will not provide Read Now clients with any type of transportation.

Professional Representation

Volunteer tutors will represent themselves as Read Now tutors, and will act appropriately in all interactions with clients.

Complaint Process

If a volunteer tutor violates this Code of Conduct, or if a client feels that a volunteer tutor has violated this code, the matter should be reported to Read Now management immediately for investigation and appropriate action.

Agreement

By submitting a volunteer application, you acknowledge that you have read and agree to follow this Code of Conduct. Failure to follow these standards may result in termination of your volunteer relationship with Read Now.
