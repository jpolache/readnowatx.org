About Us

Understanding our mission, methodology, and team

Our Story

Our founder, David Polacheck has experience in adult literacy tutoring going back to 1998, while two of our board members, Karen and Angeliska Polacheck have extensive teaching experience.

We are an organization and a family. Read Now is run by the Polachecks, a husband (David), wife (Karen), daughter (Angeliska), brother (Jon) team along with an ever growing group of volunteer tutors.

Our Methodology

Our teaching methodology is unique to Read Now and is based in part on the Learning Interactive Constructivist Principles of Dr. Dee Tadlock.

The Read Now methodology is based on the insight of Dr Dee Tadlock that silent reading is a cognitive function and not a linguistic one. To be more specific, decoding, or sounding out words is not the reading strategy that effectively helps the struggling reader.

Conceived of in 2010 to assist students in at home silent reading practice, the Silent Reading Teacher application is now an integral part of the Read Now teaching strategy.

How Our Program Works

Our unique approach focuses on developing the silent reading skill through carefully structured tutoring sessions. Each student works one-on-one with a trained volunteer tutor to:

  • Build foundational reading skills
  • Develop confidence in silent reading
  • Progress at their own pace
  • Gain practical reading proficiency

Our Impact

Read Now has served hundreds of adult learners in the Austin area. On average, our students gain one reading grade level every 3 sessions with our volunteer tutors.

The best outcomes are created one-on-one, so that's what you'll get from us every time.

Organization Status

Read Now is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization dedicated to providing free adult literacy tutoring in Austin, Texas. We accept donations and rely on volunteers to accomplish our mission.
