Student Literacy Services

Free, personalized tutoring to help you develop the silent reading skill

Is This for Me?

Silent reading is a complex activity that is normally acquired in childhood. However for a number of reasons, adults sometimes need help developing or restoring the silent reading skill. The mission of Read Now is to provide that help.

A simple way to tell if a reading problem exists is this; when a person can understand a text that is read aloud but is not able to read that same text silently with comfort, that's a reading problem. This is often not an insurmountable problem given the right help and the right tools.

What to Expect

Always Free

Tutors are always free for our students.

Local & Convenient

We're happy to meet near you at any local library in greater Austin area.

Flexible Scheduling

Session times are arranged between student and tutor.

In-Person or Remote

While sessions have traditionally taken place live, we now offer remote options too!

Proven Results

We currently average an increase of one reading grade level every 3 sessions. The best outcomes are created one-on-one, so that's what you'll get from us every time.
