Become a Volunteer Tutor

Help adults learn to read through one-on-one tutoring

Join Our Team

Our volunteer adult literacy tutors are dedicated to the idea that helping others overcome reading problems is a calling. They do this by using the Read Now literacy methodology, which helps the student to develop the silent reading skill.

Tutors work one on one with students in person at various branches of the Austin Public Library or by phone. They keep records of each students' progress and abide by the Read Now Volunteer Tutor Code of Conduct.

Volunteers receive free training and supervision from Read Now.

Why Volunteer?

Read Now is dependent on Volunteer tutors to serve our client base. The effectiveness of our methodology changes lives and is a very satisfying volunteer experience.

  • Make a real impact in your community
  • Receive free training in our proven methodology
  • Work flexible hours that fit your schedule
  • Experience the satisfaction of helping someone learn to read
  • Join a supportive community of volunteers and staff

Requirements

To become a Read Now volunteer tutor, you must meet these basic requirements:

  • Desire to make an impact
  • 18+ years of age
  • Consent to background check

Next Steps

If you are interested in becoming a Read Now volunteer tutor, please complete and submit the Volunteer Tutor Application form.

After submission, we will contact you to discuss training and placement opportunities.

Code of Conduct

All volunteers agree to follow our Volunteer Tutor Code of Conduct, which outlines our expectations for professional and ethical behavior.
